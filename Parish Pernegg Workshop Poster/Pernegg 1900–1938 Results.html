What we learned

  • Marriage and death books read well. 11197, 12287 and 8758 are 95–99% clean. Across all books the decider agreed with the book type on 2,136 of 2,140 halves it classified.
  • Baptism books are the problem. 12276, 8708 and 6346-TB-09 account for 453 of the 499 review halves. Their wide tables often come back as text blocks (417 halves) instead of a table, so those rows are not split into columns yet.
  • The decider flags 5,115 of 17,369 rows (29%) as likely filler. It catches blank and dotted lines. It does not catch invented text: one 12276 row (book year 1908) has dates in 1937 and 2007 and scored only 0.49.
  • Retries help. 753 halves were retried once; the 26 that failed on a file-write race are fixed.
  • The "ragged columns" flag is noisy (about a third of halves). Header cells are merged, so it is a note, not an error.
